FAQ

How do I win the Nozel Prize?
Make at least one deal every hour. Each hour with a deal adds a step, each hour without one removes a step. Fill all 24 steps to win. Night hours (23:00–7:00) don't count.

Why is he asleep?
He sleeps from 23:00 to 7:00. Press DEAL or DRINK to wake him up for 15 minutes. He won't like it.

Why did he make a deal by himself?
Too much fizz! If you press DRINK when he's already full, he goes hyper for 90 seconds and presses DEAL on his own. Those deals don't count toward the Nozel Prize.

Where do the deals come from?
On devices with Apple Intelligence they're written on your device. On other devices the game uses its own hand-written deals.

Is my data collected?
No. See the Privacy Policy.
