Privacy Policy

Big Red Button: Pocket Leader · Last updated 10 November 2026

Big Red Button: Pocket Leader ("the app") is a game made by Dmitry Ulyanov. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: the app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your data anywhere.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (the leader's needs, crises, deals, prizes and settings such as sound on/off) is saved only on your device. A small copy of the leader's mood and progress is shared with the app's own home-screen widget on the same device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple Intelligence

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the game asks Apple's on-device language model to write the deal options. This happens on your device. The game sends no personal information to the model, only the name of the fictional crisis, and nothing is sent to us.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders (for example "He is thirsty") locally on your device. No push server is used and no data leaves your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
